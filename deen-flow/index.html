Deen Flow

Darood Sharif, Ruhani Ilaj, Duas, and Azkar. Every wording sits in the section it belongs to, with Arabic, reading, meaning, and the Islamic reference.

Darood Sharif درود شریف Salawat he ﷺ taught, and the virtues in sound hadith. Ruhani Ilaj روحانی علاج Ruqyah: Qur'an and dua, with the cure asked from Allah alone. Duas دعائیں Supplications from the Qur'an and the Prophet's ﷺ duas. Azkar اذکار Morning, evening, after salah, sleep, and the daily dhikr.

How this library was chosen

Open frame: Sunni Islam, with Tasawwuf practiced according to Islam. Sincerity over display. This page is a reminder, not a fatwa and not a medical prescription.

What is included

  • The Qur'an, cited by surah and ayah
  • Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim
  • The four Sunan, when a hadith scholar graded the report or the wording is the familiar adhkar text
  • One composed salawat, labeled as composed, not as a hadith

What is left out

  • Talismans, number squares, and unknown scripts
  • Calling on jinn, stars, or anyone besides Allah
  • Formulas sold with a guaranteed hidden reward
  • Any wording presented as the Prophet's ﷺ when it is not

How to use it

  • Stay in one section. Darood is not mixed into the duas list.
  • Read the grade. Sahih, Hasan, Qur'an, and Composed are not the same.
  • The counter is a private daily aid. It resets on your next calendar day.
  • For a ruling, ask a qualified scholar. For an illness, see a doctor and still make dua.
