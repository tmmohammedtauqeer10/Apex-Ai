# Idea: Islamic in-app social under Sunni Islam and Tasawwuf

Assumption: this is a product brief for WorkStudio, not a fatwa. A qualified scholar must review worship, finance, and mixed-gender interaction rules before launch.

## Problem

Mainstream social feeds push noise, privacy risk, and content that conflicts with Islamic limits. People still need safe ways to share useful daily-life help with family and community.

## Intended audience

Muslim users who want privacy-first sharing, halal content, and practical daily-life benefit. Primary circle: family, local community, trusted teachers. Secondary: broader public learning posts that stay modest.

## Proposed solution

Build social as a small in-app circle, not a viral public square.

Core surfaces:

1. Private circles (family, mosque class, study group)
2. Useful daily posts (tips, reminders, charity needs, learning notes)
3. Halal content rules with reporting and review
4. Privacy controls that default to closed sharing
5. Soft Tasawwuf tone: sincerity, humility, service — no showy spirituality

## Alignment with Sunni Islam and Tasawwuf

- Privacy protects dignity and trust.
- Halal filters keep speech and media within Islamic limits.
- Useful daily-life content serves people, not vanity metrics.
- Tasawwuf guides tone: sincerity over display.

## Allowed to build now

- Private posts and circles (invite-only)
- Text posts for daily tips, study notes, charity coordination, event reminders
- Prayer-time and calendar reminders that do not invent rulings
- Content report button and moderator queue
- Block, mute, and leave-circle controls
- End-to-end or at least encrypted private messages between consented users
- Local-first drafts; export and delete my data
- Parental or family guardian mode for younger accounts
- Quiet hours and no infinite scroll / no autoplay of loud media by default
- Verified teacher or mosque pages with clear labels, no paid engagement tricks

## Needs a scholar before launch

- Public mixed-gender live sessions and open comment threads
- Any finance: donations processing, marketplace, Zakat calculators that claim a ruling
- Image and video rules for faces, voice, and mixed gatherings
- Ranking or recommendation that may push one scholar’s view over another
- Monetization: tips, ads, creator payouts
- Claims that a post is “Sunnah”, “fard”, or a binding fatwa

## Refuse or redesign

- Pornography, sexualized content, intoxicant promotion
- Interest-based lending pitches and gambling
- Fortune-telling, magic claims, or secret spiritual powers
- Mockery of religion, hate bait, and harassment
- Selling personal data or shadow profiles
- Addictive infinite feed, dark-pattern notifications, fake scarcity
- Anonymous public pile-ons

## Assumptions to test

1. Users prefer invite-only circles over a public feed.
2. Useful daily posts get more return visits than entertainment posts.
3. Clear “not a fatwa” labels reduce misuse of religious claims.
4. Family guardians accept the privacy model for younger users.
